# Data Dictionary

> ## ⚠️ ALL DATA IN THIS FOLDER IS SYNTHETIC / SIMULATED
> Generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` (seed 42) from assumptions in `config/data_generation.yaml`.
> It is **not** Sanofi or Sanofi Consumer Healthcare India Ltd data. Territory names are real cities used only as labels.
> The only exception is the *public* fields of `competitor_data.csv` (brand, marketer, strength, pack, MRP), which come from public listings documented in `research/sources.md`.

---

## 1. How the data was built

| Layer | What it controls | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Recall in March 2024, returns in April 2024, zero sales until relaunch in September 2025; 15 ml MRP ₹190; North India and winter demand slightly higher | Public facts and directional evidence (`research/market_research.md`) |
| **Magnitudes** | Cohort sizes, shares, adherence, channel mix, discounts, spend | Assumptions in `config/data_generation.yaml` |
| **Planted relationships** | A handful of cause-and-effect links for the analysis to discover | Listed in §3 below — **these are not real-world findings** |
| **Noise and data-quality issues** | Random variation, duplicates, label errors, missing values | Listed in §4 below |

Reproduce with `python scripts/generate_data.py && python scripts/validate_data.py` (27 checks).

**Headline shape of the simulated story:** by Jun–Aug 2026 the brand's seasonally adjusted monthly revenue has recovered to about **76% of its pre-recall run-rate**, and recovery has largely plateaued. That plateau is the business problem the project analyses.

## 3. Planted relationships (what the analysis *should* find)

These were **built in on purpose**. Finding them demonstrates the method; it is **not** evidence about the real market.

| # | Relationship | Strength in data | Supports question |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Stock availability ↑ → units ↑ (elasticity 0.9) | within-series r ≈ 0.30 | Q7 |
| P2 | HCP brand awareness ↑ → recommendation ↑ | r ≈ 0.54 | Q4 |
| P3 | Pre-recall prescribers recommend ~2× more | 0.36 vs 0.17 | Q3, Q11 |
| P4 | Rep visits help with diminishing returns (log effect) | modest | Q4, Q11 |
| P5 | **Patient volume has no effect on recommendation** once specialty is controlled | within-specialty \|r\| < 0.15 | Q4 |
| P6 | Rep visits only weakly aligned to patient volume (misallocated call plans) | r ≈ 0.14 | Q11 |
| P7 | Recall-aware parents trust the brand less | 3.0 vs 3.9 (of 5) | Q9 |
| P8 | Pediatrician-recommended parents adhere best | 0.71 vs ~0.55 | Q9 |
| P9 | Territory recovery depends on sales-force coverage, awareness and competitor strength, **not** on market potential | by design | Q2, Q6 |
| P10 | Within a channel, CAC rises as monthly spend rises (diminishing returns) | positive in all channels | Q10 |

## 4. Deliberate data-quality issues (for `01_data_cleaning.ipynb`)

| File | Issue | Scale | Correct handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| sales | Exact duplicate rows | 63 | Drop on (date, territory_id, channel, product) |
| sales | State label variants: "NCT of Delhi", "Tamil nadu", "UP", "maharashtra" | ~1% of rows | Map to canonical names |
| sales | Negative units/revenue in Apr 2024 | recall month | Keep, but flag as returns and exclude from demand trends |
| sales | Null `discount_pct` | off-market rows + ~1% of active rows | Leave off-market as null; impute active rows with channel-month median |
| sales | Zero rows May 2024 – Aug 2025 | off-market | Flag `market_phase`; exclude from averages |
| hcp | `monthly_patient_volume` = 9999 | 6 rows | Treat as entry error; set null or impute specialty median |
| hcp | Null `digital_engagement` | ~2% | Impute or exclude from digital analyses |
| consumer | Null `price_sensitivity` | ~1.5% | Exclude from price analyses |

## 5. Known confounders (traps to avoid and to discuss in interviews)

1. **Volume vs recommendation (raw r ≈ 0.28) is confounded by specialty.** Pediatricians have both higher volume and higher recommendation. Within each specialty the link disappears (P5).
2. **Raw stock vs units correlation (≈ 0.2) mixes large and small territories.** Normalise within territory. Stock and share also both ramp up after relaunch, so control for months since relaunch before calling it causal.
3. **CAC is not comparable across audiences.** One HCP conversion influences many patients; one parent conversion is one household. Compare within audience, or compute value-adjusted CAC.
4. **Sample ≠ universe.** `hcp_data` is a sample; scale using `territory_data.hcp_count`.
5. **Synthetic ≠ real.** Any ranking, share or ₹ figure is an output of assumptions, not a statement about DePURA Kids' actual performance.
