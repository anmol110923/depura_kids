# Industry Perspective — Usage Policy

This project draws on **general industry perspective** from a professional working at Sanofi, who is a family connection of the author. This file states in writing what that input was, and what it was not.

## What it IS used for

- **General, industry-wide context** that any experienced practitioner could share publicly, for example:
  - how pediatric OTC/consumer healthcare brands typically combine HCP detailing and pharmacy trade programs;
  - what commercial teams generally track (numeric distribution, fill rates, HCP coverage, call frequency);
  - which questions are sensible to ask of this kind of problem.
- **Sense-checking** whether a hypothesis or assumption is *plausible* in an industry context.

## What it is NOT used for

- Any Sanofi or SCHIL internal data: sales, share, targets, forecasts, prescriber lists, rep coverage, spend, pricing strategy or relaunch plans.
- Any non-public detail about the recall, the investigation, the manufacturing site or the relaunch.
- Validating or "correcting" synthetic numbers toward real ones. If a synthetic figure happens to resemble reality, that is coincidence and is not claimed.
- Endorsement. The practitioner has not reviewed or approved the strategy, and the project does not represent Sanofi's views.

## How it is recorded

- The practitioner is **not named** anywhere in the project.
- Any assumption informed by the conversation is labelled **ASSUMPTION** or **HYPOTHESIS**, never **FACT**.
- If a conversation touches something that might be non-public, it is **excluded entirely** rather than paraphrased.

## How it is disclosed

- README and case study: *"Independent case study based on publicly available information, with general industry perspective from a Sanofi professional."*
- Resume: describe it as *informed by conversations with an industry practitioner*; do not name the company or the person on the resume.
- Interview: if asked, explain the relationship openly, and state clearly that no internal information was used.

## Author checklist before sharing publicly

- [ ] The practitioner is comfortable with this reference and its wording.
- [ ] No sentence in the project could only have been written with internal knowledge.
- [ ] Every number about DePURA Kids performance is labelled synthetic or illustrative.
