# Sources Register

**Independent case study based on publicly available information, with general industry perspective from a Sanofi professional.**
This is not a Sanofi project, internship, consulting engagement or Sanofi-approved strategy. No confidential or non-public information is used.

All sources accessed **17 September 2026**. Pharmacy prices are point-in-time snapshots and differ across platforms (several listings note that MRPs were revised for GST changes and may differ from the printed label).

## Reliability tiers

| Tier | Meaning | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Official company, regulator, government statistics, peer-reviewed research | Stated as fact |
| **B** | Reputable news or financial media reporting on filings/circulars | Stated as fact, attributed |
| **C** | Online pharmacy / retailer listings | Price and pack snapshots only; strengths cross-checked; conflicts flagged |
| **D** | Low-credibility or indirect (e.g. foreign retailer, marketing blogs) | Only to flag something for verification; never a sole basis |
